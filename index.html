        Our story

        Better meals begin with what you already have.

        Why we started GoodFood

        GoodFood began when a group of students kept having the same problem: full cupboards, busy schedules, and no idea what to cook without overspending.

        We imagined a friendly meal guide that brings budget-friendly weekly dinners and grocery planning together in one place.

        ❤

        Our mission

        Make affordable, healthy eating feel simple, practical, and enjoyable for everyone.

        Thoughtful

        Recipes should respect real budgets and everyday ingredients.

        Helpful

        Finding a meal should be quick, clear, and reassuring.

        Waste-conscious

        Small changes at dinner can help food go further.
